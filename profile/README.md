# LIMNMEDIA

**Tools, technology, and education for creative production.**

LIMNMEDIA develops accessible tools and practical knowledge for stop-motion animation, visual effects, and related creative disciplines.

Our projects help artists capture, measure, move, align, and work with physical and digital environments. We develop software, hardware, open methods, and educational resources designed to make complex production techniques more approachable.

## Why LIMNMEDIA exists

Creative production often depends on specialized equipment, expensive software, fragmented documentation, and techniques that are difficult to learn independently.

LIMNMEDIA explores ways to reduce those barriers through practical tools, open knowledge, and artist-centered design.

**Stop-Motion Is Real.**

*Open, teachable, and affordable by design.*

## Projects

- **[LIMNTPS](https://github.com/Limnmedia/limntps)** — Record and recover camera setups.
- **[TPS (Three Point System)](https://github.com/Limnmedia/tps)** — An open method for preserving and reconstructing camera views.
- **[LIMNLENS](https://github.com/Limnmedia/limnlens)** — Measure focal length and support camera matching.
- **[LIMNMOCO](https://github.com/Limnmedia/limnmoco)** — Motion-control equipment for stop-motion cinematography.
- **[LIMNPOCLO (LIMNMEDIA POINT CLOUD)](https://github.com/Limnmedia/limnpoclo)** — Tools for working with 3D scans.
- **[LCRD](https://github.com/Limnmedia/lcrd)** — Reference data for cameras, sensors, and imaging hardware.
- **[LIMN-EDU](https://github.com/Limnmedia/limn-education)** — Stop-motion knowledge, terminology, and educational resources.

Projects are at different stages of research, development, and availability. Some linked repositories are private and may not be accessible to visitors.

## Our approach

**Start with the problem, not the tool.**

We believe creative technology should be understandable, adaptable, and useful to the people doing the work.
