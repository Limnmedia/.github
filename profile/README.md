# LIMNMEDIA

**Stop-Motion Is Real.**

We help filmmakers and animators bring their ideas to life.

A movie is a wonderful trick. Show a series of pictures, one after another, and suddenly something moves. Change where the camera looks, and you change what the audience sees. Put two shots together, and you can tell a story without saying a word.

That's the magic of filmmaking. More people should have the chance to explore it.

## Our mission

Making movies can get expensive and complicated fast. Equipment costs money. Software takes time to learn. Useful techniques can be hard to find. Sometimes filmmakers spend more time figuring out how to make a shot than making it.

**We want filmmakers to spend less time fighting the process and more time telling their stories.**

LIMNMEDIA starts with the problems artists face. We develop practical methods, tools, equipment, and educational resources to make animation, cinematography, and visual effects easier to learn and more achievable.

**The solution is in the problem, not the tools.**

*Open, teachable, and affordable by design.*

## Projects

- **[LIMNTPS](https://github.com/Limnmedia/limntps)** — Record a camera setup so you can find the same view again.
- **[TPS](https://github.com/Limnmedia/tps)** — Recover a camera view using three reference points.
- **[LIMNLENS](https://github.com/Limnmedia/limnlens)** — Understand focal length and match what a camera sees.
- **[LIMNMOCO](https://github.com/Limnmedia/limnmoco)** — Make repeatable camera moves for stop-motion.
- **[LIMNPOCLO](https://github.com/Limnmedia/limnpoclo)** — Inspect, clean, and align 3D scans.
- **[LCRD](https://github.com/Limnmedia/lcrd)** — Find reference information about cameras and sensors.
- **[LIMN-EDU](https://github.com/Limnmedia/limn-education)** — Learn the language and techniques of stop-motion.

These projects are at different stages of development. Some repositories are private and may not be accessible to visitors.

## Where we come from

Filmmaking began with curious people experimenting with pictures, light, cameras, and movement. Long before computers and giant studios, they invented tricks with what they had.

Stop-motion is one of those tricks: move something a little, take a picture, and do it again. Play the pictures back, and it comes alive.

More than a century later, that simple idea still works. We're here to help more people try it.
